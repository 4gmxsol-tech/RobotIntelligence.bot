PRODUCT OVERVIEW

A public-signal research queue, not a buyer database

Human-reviewed intelligence

Buyer Radar is a browser-based research interface for exploring public robotics and Physical AI market signals. Set an asset name and a market query, then scan public GitHub repository metadata and Hacker News stories surfaced through Algolia. The interface organizes source-linked evidence into signal categories, possible market triggers, heuristic organization candidates and a research brief.

  1. Define the research target: enter the asset or product being evaluated and the robotics market topics that matter.
  2. Discover public signals: search GitHub repository metadata and Hacker News stories, retaining links and dates for review.
  3. Review the evidence: inspect signal context, trigger type, possible organization matches and the reasons a candidate was surfaced.
  4. Verify independently: confirm the company, current need, decision-maker, buying authority and any acquisition trigger before taking action.

Organization matching, event classification and the 0–100 research-priority score are heuristic. A candidate is a research lead, not a verified buyer. The score does not measure budget, purchasing authority, acquisition intent, asset ownership, sale authorization, availability or transfer scope.

The scanner does not send email, submit contact forms or contact organizations. It produces a human-reviewed research queue only. Any outreach requires separate verification of the recipient, sender identity and applicable legal requirements.

09 / SCORING METHOD

What the priority score means

Heuristic research aid · not purchase intent

The 0–100 priority ranking is a heuristic built from four evidence signals. It helps sort research opportunities; it is not a probability of purchase, a company valuation, or proof that a company wants to acquire an asset.

01

Topical relevance

Measures overlap between the company and public signal text, the asset name, and the market query.

02

Signal recency

Counts evidence from the last 90 days. The strongest event is labelled fresh at 30 days or less, recent at 31–90 days, or older.

03

Commercial activity

Recognizes public signals such as funding, investment, acquisitions, partnerships, launches, contracts, customers, orders, and expansion.

04

Evidence coverage

Counts distinct public evidence items, with a cap so raw volume cannot dominate the ranking.

What the score does not establish: It does not measure a company's budget, ability or authority to buy, acquisition intent, or the ownership, sale authorization, availability, and transfer scope of an asset.

Source and outreach limits: The scanner currently searches public GitHub and Hacker News signals. Entity resolution and event classification are heuristic. A human must verify every source, date, company, relevant team, decision-maker channel, sender identity, and applicable outreach rules. The tool does not send messages or contact companies.
