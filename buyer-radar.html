09 / SCORING METHOD

What the priority score means

Heuristic research aid · not purchase intent

The 0–100 priority ranking is a heuristic built from four evidence signals. It helps sort research opportunities; it is not a probability of purchase, a company valuation, or proof that a company wants to acquire an asset.

01

Topical relevance

Measures overlap between the company and public signal text, the asset name, and the market query.

02

Signal recency

Counts evidence from the last 90 days. The strongest event is labelled fresh at 30 days or less, recent at 31–90 days, or older.

03

Commercial activity

Recognizes public signals such as funding, investment, acquisitions, partnerships, launches, contracts, customers, orders, and expansion.

04

Evidence coverage

Counts distinct public evidence items, with a cap so raw volume cannot dominate the ranking.

What the score does not establish: It does not measure a company's budget, ability or authority to buy, acquisition intent, or the ownership, sale authorization, availability, and transfer scope of an asset.

Source and outreach limits: The scanner currently searches public GitHub and Hacker News signals. Entity resolution and event classification are heuristic. A human must verify every source, date, company, relevant team, decision-maker channel, sender identity, and applicable outreach rules. The tool does not send messages or contact companies.
